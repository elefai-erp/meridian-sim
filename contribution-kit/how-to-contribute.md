# How to Contribute a Scenario — Guide for CA / Finance Professionals

**You do not need GitHub. You do not need YAML. You do not need to install anything.**
You write in plain English through a web form; everything technical happens
automatically after you hit Submit.

## What you're contributing

A **scenario** — one real-world accounting situation from your practice or industry
experience, adapted into the world of Meridian Trading Pvt Ltd (a fictional Hyderabad
electrical goods trading company). Students face your scenario without knowing it's a
test: an email arrives, a document lands, books don't balance — and they must handle it
the way a real accountant would.

## Before you write (15 minutes of reading)

1. **[The Company Bible summary](../company-bible/meridian-trading.md)** — the company, the people, the vendors, the world rules. Your scenario must fit this world.
2. **[Rajan's voice guide](rajan-character-guide.md)** — every scenario includes the bad advice Rajan gives when the student asks for help. He must sound like Rajan.
3. **Two example scenarios** — see [`scenarios/gst/`](../scenarios/gst/) and [`scenarios/tds/`](../scenarios/tds/) for the standard your submission will be edited into.

## The form (20 minutes of writing)

The submission form asks you, in plain English:

1. **About you** — name, CA/CMA membership number, specialisation, city, years of practice
2. **The situation** — what happens? Write it like you're telling a colleague about a client incident
3. **The task** — what should the student do? What does a lazy/wrong student typically do? What is the correct treatment, and **which section/rule/circular says so**?
4. **Rajan's bad advice** — what would a vague or outdated manager say when asked? Why is it wrong?
5. **The consequence** — if the student gets it wrong, what happens later, and roughly when does it surface?
6. **Supporting files (optional)** — an invoice format, a notice copy, a statement extract that inspired this. We anonymise these and drop them into the student's DriveBox.

The form link is shared in the contributor WhatsApp group each month with that month's
theme.

## What happens after you submit

1. WhatsApp confirmation: *"Your scenario is under review"*
2. A **Domain Reviewer** — a qualified CA/CMA with 5+ years in your scenario's domain — checks the technical accuracy, the law references, and whether Rajan's advice is believably wrong. They may come back with questions; please respond within 7 days.
3. On approval, your scenario is tested with a small student cohort for 2 weeks, then goes live for everyone.
4. WhatsApp announcement with your name: *"Your scenario is now active."*

Every live scenario permanently carries:

```
Authored-by:  CA <Your Name> (ICAI M.No. XXXXXX)
Reviewed-by:  CA <Reviewer Name> (ICAI M.No. XXXXXX)
```

## Your ongoing responsibilities

- **Accuracy:** wrong advice published means students learn wrong — that's why review exists, but the first responsibility is yours
- **Currency:** when the law changes under your scenario (a circular, a rate revision, an amendment), you'll be asked to update it. Scenarios not updated within 90 days of a flagged law change are taken offline until re-verified
- **Responsiveness:** reply to reviewer feedback within 7 days

## What you receive

- Named credit (with membership number) on every scenario, on the platform and in this repository
- A LinkedIn-shareable contributor badge and platform profile page
- Monthly recognition in the contributor community — including which scenario stumped the most students
- A path to **Domain Reviewer** (after ~5 quality scenarios, by invitation) and **Core Contributor**

## Eligibility

- CA / CMA / CS — any stage (pursuing or qualified), **or** B.Com/M.Com with 3+ years practical accounting experience
- Your first submission is reviewed as a work sample; full contributor access follows once it passes
- You must have personally completed the Level 2 simulation before contributing — so you know what the student experiences
