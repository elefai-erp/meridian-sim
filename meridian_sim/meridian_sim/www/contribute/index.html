Three contributor roles

Entry level

Scenario Author

Write scenario cards — situations, tasks, hidden errors, consequences, and your manager character's deliberately bad advice, with the correct treatment and law reference.

Eligibility: CA/CMA/CS (any stage) or B.Com/M.Com with 3+ years' experience. Your first scenario is your work sample.

Invitation only

Domain Reviewer

The quality gate. Owns one domain entirely — nothing goes live in GST, TDS, Payroll, or five other domains without their sign-off.

Eligibility: CA/CMA qualified, 5+ years post-qualification in the domain. Nominated by the Core Team.

Invitation only · max 7

Core Contributor

Shapes curriculum structure, level design, and character behaviour. Maintains the Company Bible. Resolves disputes.

Eligibility: 10+ approved scenarios authored, 20+ reviews completed, 1-year commitment.

The progression path

Anyone → Scenario Author (after your sample scenario passes review) → Domain Reviewer (invitation, after ~5 approved scenarios + demonstrated domain depth) → Core Contributor (invitation, after 10 authored + 20 reviewed + long-term commitment). No one joins as Core Contributor on day one — not even founding team members.
