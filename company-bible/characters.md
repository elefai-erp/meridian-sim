# Character Profiles — The Cast of Meridian Trading

> **Status:** Draft v0.1 — pending Core Team sign-off.
> Every character has a personality, an agenda, and a communication style. They are not
> task generators — they are people with their own pressures. Scenario authors must keep
> every character consistent with this document. Rajan has his own detailed voice guide:
> [contribution-kit/rajan-character-guide.md](../contribution-kit/rajan-character-guide.md).

## Rajan Mehta — Senior Accounts Manager (the boss)

The most important character in the simulation.

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| Role | Senior Accounts Manager — the student's direct boss |
| Age / experience | 47. 22 years in accounts. Tally 4.0 era. Joined Meridian in 2014 |
| Channels | PingDesk (mostly) + InboxPro |
| Communication style | Vague, rushed, assumes you know everything. No formats, no deadlines spelled out |
| Availability | Replies in 2–3 hours. Sometimes not at all |
| Teaching style | Zero. Shows, never explains |
| Key trait | **Occasionally wrong — and the student must catch it** |
| Arc | Month 1 relaxed → Month 2 stressed (audit notice; starts deflecting blame) → Month 3 absent (in auditor meetings; replies once every 2 days) |

Rajan's bad advice is the simulation's core mechanic. It must be *believably* wrong —
outdated practice, oversimplification, or a shortcut that creates audit risk — never
cartoonishly wrong, and never dangerous beyond the simulation's scope. See the voice
guide before writing a single line of his dialogue.

## Writing rules for all characters

1. **Characters never explain accounting.** They have their own jobs. Explanations come from the student's own research and AI usage — that's the pedagogy.
2. **Characters have memory.** If the student promised Suresh payment by Friday, Suresh follows up on Monday referencing that promise. Scenario authors should write follow-up variants.
3. **Escalations flow upward realistically.** Vendor → Rajan → MD. HR → Rajan. Auditor → Rajan → MD. The student feels an escalation before it reaches the MD only if they're paying attention.
4. **Tone is professional-Indian-workplace.** Hinglish is fine on PingDesk ("bhai", "na", "kya hua"), never in auditor or MD emails.
5. **No new named characters** without Core Team approval. The world stays small so continuity stays manageable.
